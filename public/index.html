Passive Perception

Pass secret notes to players at the table, from the DM's phone to theirs. No app, no accounts.

Your rooms

How it works

  1. Prepare cards Clues, handouts, maps. Or type a quick whisper at the table.
  2. Players join They scan a code with their phone and pick their name. Nothing to install, no account.
  3. Tap and reveal Pick who gets it. Only their phones receive it; nobody else at the table can see it.

Why it's different

  • Secrets stay secret. The server sends each phone only what was revealed to it, so there's nothing hidden in the page for a curious player to find.
  • One-way, on purpose. No chat, no dice, no maps. The talking stays at the table.
  • Free and open source. Run your own copy on a laptop, a home server or a free Cloudflare account.
